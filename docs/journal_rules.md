# Journal rules

## Chosen journal: Public Health (Elsevier; Royal Society for Public Health), hybrid

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/public-health/publish/guide-for-authors
Accessed: 19 November 2026. Article type: Original Research.

- Length: at most 3,000 words.
- Exhibits: at most 5 tables and figures together in the manuscript; further ones go online as supplementary material.
- References: at most 50; AMA style.
- Abstract: structured, at most 250 words (Objectives, Study design, Methods, Results, Conclusions).
- Keywords: 3 to 6.
- Review: double-blind. An anonymised manuscript plus a separate title page.
- Cover letter: must say why Public Health, what is already known, what the study adds, and that the paper is not published elsewhere.
- Language: British English.
- Figures: TIFF or JPEG at 300 dpi or more, or vector EPS or PDF.
- Tables: no vertical rules; footnotes as superscript lowercase letters.
- Section "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process" before the References.
- Preprints are allowed. A data statement is encouraged.

## Fallback journal: Indian Journal of Tuberculosis (hybrid)

Rules are fetched only if the fallback is needed; none are recorded yet.
